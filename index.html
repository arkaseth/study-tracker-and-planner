Welcome to Estudio

A calm, local-first study planner built entirely around active recall and spaced repetition.

Let's take a quick 4-step tour to see how to study effectively.

▤

The 14-Day Plan

Tell Estudio your daily availability. It will automatically generate an adaptive 14-day schedule that prioritizes your weakest topics and prevents burnout.

◌

The Review Queue

Every day, you'll get a batch of flashcards to review. Rate your recall honestly—Estudio uses a spaced repetition algorithm so hard cards come back sooner.

AI & OCR Copilot

Stuck on a problem? Paste a screenshot to extract the text, or ask the Bring-Your-Own-Key AI to instantly generate missing concepts for you.
